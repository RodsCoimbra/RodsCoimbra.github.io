About me

Practical autonomy for robots that work beyond the lab.

I am a robotics engineer focused on the software that lets robots perceive, navigate, plan, and act in real environments. My work combines ROS 2 systems engineering, navigation and manipulation pipelines, and research in reinforcement-learning-based motion planning. I value clear interfaces, disciplined experimentation, and software that is understandable, testable, and useful to the people who build on it.
